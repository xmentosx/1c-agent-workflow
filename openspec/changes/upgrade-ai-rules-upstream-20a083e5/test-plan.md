## Migration acceptance (IM7, D11)

Use the existing installed update/snapshot owner and owned isolated canaries;
qualification starts from the published workflow baseline, retaining r36 and
supported old states as applicable additional cases. Do not patch installed env,
receipts or lifecycle state to manufacture readiness. Record exact old/new source,
fork, client set, root provenance, before/result setting and snapshot identity.

| Scenario | Required observation |
|---|---|

the refusal and qualify the original update/recovery task through its supported
continuation; do not weaken the guard or remove the conflict to hide it.

These scenarios are acceptance requirements, not reports of executed migration.

their exact identities and limitations. They do not establish live recovery from

## Behavioural acceptance (EV9, D4.1)

After a separately authorized important UI change reaches an owned dev/test base,
capture a fresh managed ITL Vanessa UI expected/actual trace on the exact artifact
and target. Record effective UI/provider policy and actual observed steps/result.
Prove independence from `ITL_VANESSA_TESTING`, including a retained off value;
policy migration itself is not permission to deploy or run saved suites.

Check that essential limits automatic work to important new/changed user-visible
behaviour, while auto/manual/off retain their upstream meanings and the broader
no-UI instruction retains precedence. An unavailable or unauthorized route leaves
the scenario unverified with a precise prerequisite; an explicit permitted UI
request blocks at that prerequisite. Static checks, scenario files or a generic
saved-suite pass cannot replace real interactive evidence. The repeated test-fix
loop remains opt-in. Standalone QA is not implemented or qualified by these runs.

Reuse existing proof only for unchanged owner inputs and an explicitly applicable
acceptance contract. Record new identity and limitations rather than promoting
historical 9ec/source/host evidence to c1 qualification. Close the new tasks only
from actual results; preserve the previous failures, checkbox history and all
remaining unverified client/provider variants.
